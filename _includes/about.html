Background & Engineering Philosophy

About Me

Bridging the entire robotics stack: from silicon and deterministic RTOS firmware to intelligent control algorithms.

The Self-Taught Foundation

My journey in technology began in junior school. Driven by pure curiosity, I started writing Python games and backend services. That early foundation evolved into a passion for systems programming. I fell in love with technical books—spending countless hours reading through classic and modern C++ literature, discrete mathematics, and computer architecture.

This insatiable appetite for understanding what happens underneath the compiler led me straight into bare-metal registers, embedded microcontrollers, and real-time operating systems.

Algorithmic Rigor Meets Physical Hardware

I view competitive programming as high-intensity mental athletics. Competing on platforms like Open Kattis, I cultivate deep instincts in Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA)—graph theory, dynamic programming, combinatorics, and computational geometry.

Rather than treating algorithms as abstract puzzles, I directly channel them into cyber-physical robotics: designing microsecond lock-free ring buffers, optimizing forward/inverse kinematics solvers, formulating LQR and energy-shaping controllers, and building zero-copy binary serialization engines.

Mechatronics & Real-World Impact

Currently pursuing a B.Eng. in Mechatronics Engineering at Lagos State University of Science and Technology (LASUSTECH), I have had the privilege to prove my engineering skills in high-stakes environments:

  • Contributing directly to ros-controls as a core member and contributor to Zenbedded, bringing Zephyr RTOS into ROS 2.
  • Winning 1st Place at the Cavista Technologies Hackathon 2025 (₦2M prize with Team Stark Underflow) and interning at Cavista Technologies.
  • Securing 1st Place at CodeFest Nigeria 2025 NIGHACK for the Vitalink wearable remote vitals IoT system.
