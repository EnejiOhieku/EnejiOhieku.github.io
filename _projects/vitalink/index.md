## 🏆 Double 1st-Place Award-Winning Health Platform

**Vitalink** is a rapid-response IoT remote vital monitoring system engineered to bridge critical telemetry gaps in distributed healthcare. The project secured consecutive top honors at two premier national hackathons:

1. **Cavista Technologies Hackathon 2025 (1st Place Champion):**
   - Led *Team Stark Underflow* (alongside Chidi Moses Ituma, Joshua Ituma Onyeka, and Emmanuel Iseoluwa Makanjuola) to claim the ₦2,000,000 top award at Yaba College of Technology, Lagos.
   - Designed the high-throughput Django backend, patient triage algorithms, and telemetry ingestion pipeline.
2. **CodeFest Nigeria 2025 NIGHACK (1st Place Champion):**
   - In the second iteration, prototyped a physical IoT wearable streaming continuous biometric pulse-oximetry and temperature readings over Wi-Fi directly to the cloud dashboard with TensorFlow anomaly classification.
